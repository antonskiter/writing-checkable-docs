# Where the rows are mis-drawn

Each entry is a shape a row is mis-drawn to — firing where it must stay silent, or sparing what it must fire on — with the clause that decides it. Every entry was a graded failure before it was a clause — the S1 and M1 entries over one document, the kanon README; the S3 and R1 entries over a later rework's cold reads. Consult the entry for the row you doubt; it is enough.

## S1 — a sentence judged by its cost in words {#s1-budget}

A reducer given a word budget and no job list cut the doc to 187 words and kept the worst parts: pruning without J0 optimizes words, not jobs. The clause: S1 decides by hired-by alone. No sentence fires for making the doc long, and none is spared for being one of few — a long doc whose every sentence a J0 job hires passes S1 entire, and the 187-word cut's defect surfaced as S2 omissions, not S1 fires. **Silent on length, both ways** — and a word budget never stands in for running the check.

## S1 — a fact harvested from tests or source {#s1-provenance}

A rewriter given a preservation checklist and no vantage gate produced an 1845-word expansion (2.7×), importing implementation and fixture facts — a manual stuffed into a README. Two clauses, one per direction. A checklist entry is not a hire: "the checklist names it" answers no J0 job, so the sentence fires however faithfully it was preserved. But provenance is no verdict either: a fact first read out of a test still passes when a J0 job hires it and it would survive deletion of the test suite — tests are the richest legible statement of behavior, and mining them is not the defect. **Silent on origin, firing on unhired** — the fixture-deletion test decides.

## S1 — a vacuous sentence in the house register {#s1-register}

Three rewrite loops spared four sentences because each wore the doc's own apodictic register — a tautology ("either an install target or is not installed at all"), a self-evident inverse ("removal is deleting what it copied"), a source header restated with a closure claim ("copies the two trees, and does nothing else"). The row is drawn to sparing rule-shaped confidence: the skill's own exemplars wear the same register, and a judge matches the register instead of running the check. The clause: the register asserts nothing — S1 decides by hired-by alone, and a sentence restating a source header, inverting a stated fact, or exhausting cases ("either X or not X") fires however sure it sounds. **Silent on register, firing on unhired.**

## S3 — a restatement bought by the cold read {#s3-cold-read}

Two cold-read agents, independent and fresh, ranked the same pointer-only sentence worst: first-pass comprehension needed the pointed-to fact, and the pointer alone sent the reader out of the doc mid-first-screen. The row is drawn to firing on the fix — a small restatement beside the pointer looks exactly like the second home S3 exists to catch. The clause: a restatement is bought, never assumed — spared only when both readers rank the pointer-only sentence worst, priced at what first-pass comprehension needs, no more. The pointer stays the home for everything else the source states; one reader, or no ranking, and the row fires as written. **Silent on the bought restatement, firing on every unbought second home.**

## M1 — a true fact under a stronger register {#m1}

An expansion pass upgraded measured facts to required ones — four falsehoods from register alone, no underlying fact touched. The row is drawn to sparing these because each underlying fact is true; the clause runs the other way: the artifact decides, and the truth of the underlying fact spares nothing. "Takes about 30 s" beside a recorded measurement **clears**; "must complete in 30 s" with nothing that refuses fires, and the fix is the register — downgrade to what the artifact supports, not delete a true fact.

## R1 — a pause on the system's own term {#r1-house-term}

The cold-read agent knows the tool genre, not the system, so every house name costs it a pause, and the row is drawn to firing on each one — a triage that counts those pauses as defects renames what the doc must keep. The clause: triage is the writer's, against A0 — a stumble the declared audience would share is a defect; a pause on the system's own term at or below the knowledge floor is not, and the term stays (W7). Rank decides the rest: a playback misreading outranks any stumble, and a playback gap at a deliberate pointer is a pass. **Silent on the system's name at or below the floor, firing on what the declared audience would share.**
